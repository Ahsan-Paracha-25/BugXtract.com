Before we get started

Practical answers.
Clear expectations.

Good QA starts with an agreed scope. We make coverage, reporting, and release risks visible from the beginning.

Is manual testing your main focus?

Yes. Human-led manual testing is our core service across web, mobile, desktop, and APIs. Automation is available on request when project scale and repeatable workflows justify it.

How is a project plan different from a retainer?

Project packages cover an agreed launch, release, or set of modules. Retainers reserve a monthly pool of hours for recurring testing and ongoing product development.

What will our developers receive?

Actionable bug reports with reproduction steps, expected and actual results, severity, and evidence, plus a summary of tested areas and remaining risks.

Can you test every device and every scenario?

Coverage is agreed around your users, budget, and product risks. We define a practical browser and device matrix and clearly report limitations. Testing reduces risk; it cannot guarantee defect-free software.

Do you offer specialist testing?

We can discuss additional manual checks during scoping. Specialist security audits, load testing, and formal accessibility certification require a separate agreed engagement.
